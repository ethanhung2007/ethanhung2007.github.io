Career Goals

Right now, my main focus is deepening my understanding of digital hardware. I'm still deciding between digital design, design verification and computer architecture. Everything in digital hardware fascinates me, so I'm trying to learn every part of the field at once.

Digital Design

What draws me most to digital design is the problem-solving process of turning a high-level architecture into synthesizable RTL. Of my current career directions, this is the one I know best, from taking Digital Systems Design class to projects such as my SIMT GPU core. I know I still have a lot to learn, though, especially how to write RTL for specialized constraints such as low-power ASICs or low-latency FPGA systems. To keep learning, I plan to continue building projects for more specialized circumstances and to take more advanced digital design and VLSI courses.

Design Verification

Design verification is the field I'm newest to, yet I'm quickly finding it just as interesting as the others. Before I verified anything, I never realized how rigorous the verification process is. Most of my learning so far has come from SiliconJackets, where I'm getting more involved and taking on more modules. For example, I recently started building a testbench for an AXI4-Lite bus functional model. Alongside that, I've been working through some UVM specific courses and reading more about formal verification through textbooks.

Computer Architecture

Computer architecture is probably the area I find most fascinating. I find the process of reasoning through design tradeoffs and building performance models very enjoyable. My research at National Taiwan University, where I built a compute-in-memory inference simulator, provided me my first practical experience for architectural modeling. To further my understanding, I'm hoping to join computer architecture focused labs here at Georgia Tech, with the goal of contributing as an undergraduate researcher.

Next Steps

Over the next few years, I want to build experience in all three areas through internships, research and SiliconJackets. My immediate goal is a hardware internship in summer 2027 in digital design, verification or FPGA engineering. Working in one of these roles in industry will give me a better grasp on which direction I should specialize in. In the long run, I want to be working at a company like Apple, AMD or Nvidia, designing the next generation of devices to further improve the field of computing.
