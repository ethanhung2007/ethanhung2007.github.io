About

More about me

I'm a second-year computer engineering major at Georgia Tech with the threads Systems & Architecture and Computing Hardware & Emerging Architectures. Most notably, over the summer I did research at National Taiwan University, where I worked on incorporating an in-memory compute module into a heterogeneous SoC simulator. The goal was to see how LLM inference can be optimized on a mobile SoC, and whether there are benefits to having in-memory compute as part of the SoC instead of an external AI accelerator. I also recently joined SiliconJackets as a design verification member, where I'm starting to implement testbenches using UVM. Beyond functional verification, I'm looking forward to learning other verification techniques like formal verification. I'm extremely interested in chip design, from digital design and verification to computer architecture, and I'm looking for opportunities to keep building hardware and learning from people who do it well.

From UIUC to Georgia Tech

I've probably been asked this question 20 times this semester alone. Quite frankly, the main reason I transferred is that I found Urbana-Champaign way too cold, with far too many cornfields. Nonetheless, I still enjoyed my first year at Illinois and recognize how much I learned and grew from my experience there. So far, I've found my transition to Georgia Tech to be quite smooth, and I'm looking forward to continuing to discover more about this school and build out my connections.
